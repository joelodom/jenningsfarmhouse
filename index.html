Welcome to The Dave Williams Farmhouse

Nestled on a working crawfish and rice farm just outside of Jennings, Louisiana, this charming farmhouse offers a peaceful escape surrounded by open land and quiet country views. The home comfortably sleeps up to eight. Downstairs features a classic farmhouse feel while upstairs offers a modern style. Relax on the screened porches, grill out, gather around the fire pit, or enjoy family games. This is a place to slow down and unwind, not to rush.

FAQ

Quick answers

How far is it from Jennings?
About ten minutes to downtown Jennings.

Is the Wi-Fi good for work and streaming?
Wi-fi is reliable and covers the entire property well, offering gigabit speed downstream.

Pets allowed?
Pets are allowed. We trust our guests to be respectful of the property with their pets.

Check-in / check-out?
Check-in 3:00 PM • Check-out 11:00 AM.

Events or visitors?
The house sleeps eight comfortably in its six beds. There is also a sleeper sofa and babies are welcome if you have a crib for them. The limiting factor for the number of guests is the two bathroom setup and a relatively small kitchen. Visitors are allowed.
